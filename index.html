One rocket, built from scratch.

Every year the American Rocketry Challenge asks teams to hit an exact altitude and flight time with a payload on board. We design ours ourselves: sketch it, simulate it, build it, then fly it and find out how wrong the simulation was.

Miss the target by a few feet and you lose points, so this is mostly a game of small decisions.

    More than rockets.

    We're a student-run club for the next generation of scientists, engineers, and space enthusiasts. Every other week we meet for hands-on STEM activities, and we take on bigger projects together.
